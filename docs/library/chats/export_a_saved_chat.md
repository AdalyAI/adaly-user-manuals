# Export a Saved Chat to PDF or PPT

1\. Hover over the plus icon to open the navigation menu

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/ea649474-3136-4a9d-8a33-6be3eb1ea240/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,153&br_px=2248,1410&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)


2\. Click the Library icon.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/ea649474-3136-4a9d-8a33-6be3eb1ea240/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=2248,1256&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=0,121)


3\. Click on the (...) of the chat you want to export.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/17d086c4-9ba1-4e57-8ac1-10208402584a/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,63&br_px=2248,1320&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=732,277)


4\. Click "Export"

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/b2e13de1-2639-47cf-a998-357ca01affdb/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,307&br_px=2248,1564&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=788,359)


5\. Click "PDF" or "PPT"

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/9f800da5-b18f-4774-9065-daf1a0b1842b/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,207&br_px=2248,1464&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=498,277)
