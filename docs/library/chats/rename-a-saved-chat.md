# Rename a Saved Chat


1\. Hover over the plus icon to open the navigation menu

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/bb5a0e26-225f-4331-a73b-4f717ab7d31d/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,153&br_px=2248,1410&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)


2\. Click the Library icon.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/bb5a0e26-225f-4331-a73b-4f717ab7d31d/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=2248,1256&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=0,123)


3\. Click on the (...) of the chat you want to rename.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/9bb6fd83-6358-4495-9b67-0f3149182124/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,62&br_px=2248,1319&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=524,277)


4\. Click "Rename"

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/8702007d-1aaa-4ff0-a439-27ce72053f35/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,297&br_px=2248,1554&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=575,277)


5\. Click the "Enter thread name" field.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/6c7c24dd-bc3b-434f-a06e-b8cc79286fd2/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,147&br_px=2248,1404&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=523,277)


6\. Type your new Chat name.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/35b33468-334e-4760-b571-7066238d50cf/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=2248,1564&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)


7\. Click "Save"

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/5bd25aa4-99c1-4816-b7f1-96d72dee05b4/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,307&br_px=2248,1564&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=795,291)
