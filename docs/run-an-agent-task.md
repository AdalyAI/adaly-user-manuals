4\. Click "Select an option"

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/4d6b6829-0c91-4e01-8c04-abbac7c2b786/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,307&br_px=2248,1564&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=424,323)

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/c8ef20e5-6575-42ba-aa93-9b19a133d28c/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,307&br_px=2248,1564&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=382,406)

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/885ff399-c42b-4167-aecd-021fdb7befb2/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,307&br_px=2248,1564&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=1014,517)

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/4676d681-202d-46cd-8426-fa902bbf6b1b/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=2248,1256&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=603,187)


8\. Select the brand you want to run this task on

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/2a468e00-cf42-4e91-8a2c-cdab6343f849/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,101&br_px=2248,1358&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=447,277)

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/116a1fb5-4fbb-455d-ab8a-a007fc7f5431/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,307&br_px=2248,1564&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=432,351)

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/571bb140-26e6-44e3-a082-0cc73ad9b995/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,307&br_px=2248,1564&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=411,438)


11\. Click "Next Step"

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/564a5af4-6510-45f3-90eb-4e620a2cac4d/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,307&br_px=2248,1564&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=1030,517)


12\. Click the "Add context..." field.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/b4fee709-a601-4dab-b47f-6618ff4d612a/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,307&br_px=2248,1564&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=826,520)


13\. Enter your primary focus (e.g "I want to make a comprehensive report for the competitors of Adidas specifically Nike")


14\. Press Enter or click the arrow icon to submit your response.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/40fb4e18-4d87-4979-a77e-de69db55ba4e/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,307&br_px=2248,1564&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=885,527)


15\. Click the "Add context..." field.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/e6607a78-546f-448d-8a01-445e228eddd4/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,307&br_px=2248,1564&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=822,525)


16\. Indicate that this is the correct focus (e.g "Yes")


17\. Click the "Add context..." field.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/d0c98114-27f8-4673-aa9b-75ee4b0a6f98/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,307&br_px=2248,1564&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=821,523)


18\. Type "Confirm" and press Enter


19\. Click the "Add context..." field.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/469787f8-ecfa-4f9b-9579-a6a52b224cb6/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,307&br_px=2248,1564&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=734,508)


20\. Type "Confirm" and press Enter


21\. Click "Run Agent"
