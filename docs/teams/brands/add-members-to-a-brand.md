1\. Hover over the plus icon to open the navigation menu

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/c40f0adc-d445-4e16-9a3e-e9d388bb1be6/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=2248,1256&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=14,204)
