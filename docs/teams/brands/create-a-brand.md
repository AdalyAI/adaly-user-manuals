# Create a Brand

1\. Hover over the plus icon to open the navigation menu

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/2adbf86c-0b03-41a7-8c85-a0d97b008e07/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,153&br_px=2248,1410&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)


2\. Click the Teams icon.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/2adbf86c-0b03-41a7-8c85-a0d97b008e07/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=2248,1256&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=0,198)

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/fc8a9bde-6790-431c-b271-31ed97f2f5d4/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,307&br_px=2248,1564&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=205,515)

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/58584577-fd1e-4bdb-aef8-12428326ab1b/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,143&br_px=2248,1400&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=421,277)


5\. Enter the name of your brand.


6\. Click "Next"

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/1eaf7e75-7d69-4662-9602-75184a9d3397/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,307&br_px=2248,1564&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=796,391)

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/ac939593-917f-4590-8f5d-b4832577e7fc/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,307&br_px=2248,1564&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=796,573)
