1\. Hover over the plus icon to open the navigation menu

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/7084f645-5ee8-4133-b458-8c62843e0d4b/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,153&br_px=2248,1410&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)


2\. Click the Teams icon.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/7084f645-5ee8-4133-b458-8c62843e0d4b/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=2248,1256&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=14,201)

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/8618acf0-e76c-4a5f-91a6-51b05c4312c3/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,307&br_px=2248,1564&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=194,284)

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/5a2e809d-5dec-49b5-9c64-c19963f8f509/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,139&br_px=2248,1396&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=932,277)
