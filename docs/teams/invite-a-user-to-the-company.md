# Invite a User to the Company

1\. Hover over the plus icon to open the navigation menu

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/e933374c-1997-414b-ad42-9ff8a4724c3a/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,153&br_px=2248,1410&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0)


2\. Click the Teams icon.

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/e933374c-1997-414b-ad42-9ff8a4724c3a/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=2248,1256&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=1,211)

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/5cbcdf1e-afcf-4657-ad3c-f24d1867ebf7/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=2248,1256&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=925,149)

![](https://ajeuwbhvhr.cloudimg.io/https://colony-recorder.s3.amazonaws.com/files/2025-10-15/3dd211a6-69b4-49cf-b6c9-367bdcb91555/ascreenshot.jpeg?tl_px=0,0&br_px=2248,1256&force_format=jpeg&q=100&width=1120.0&wat=1&wat_opacity=0.7&wat_gravity=northwest&wat_url=https://colony-recorder.s3.us-west-1.amazonaws.com/images/watermarks/FB923C_standard.png&wat_pad=650,200)

6\. Click "Invite"
